Workflow & Automation

Triggers, Notifications & Approvals

Automate routine tasks with triggers, notifications, and approval workflows. Keep your team informed and processes moving without manual intervention.

See It In Action View All Modules

Overview

Rover's Workflow module automates the routine tasks that slow your team down. From simple email notifications to complex multi-step approval processes, Rover keeps work flowing without manual intervention.

Built for growing manufacturers, workflows are easy to configure without coding. Set up triggers based on business events, route approvals to the right people, and ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

Capabilities

Key Features

Everything you need for process automation.

Event Triggers

Automate actions based on system events. New order received? PO over limit? Inventory below threshold? Trigger automatic responses.

Email Notifications

Send customizable email alerts when conditions are met. Include relevant data and links back to the system.

In-App Alerts

Push notifications directly in Rover. Your team sees alerts without leaving their workflow.

Approval Workflows

Multi-step approvals with escalation. Route to the right approver based on amount, type, or department.

Task Management

Assign and track tasks across teams. Due dates, priorities, and completion tracking all included.

Scheduled Actions

Run processes on a schedule. Daily reports, weekly summaries, monthly cleanups—all automated.

Conditional Logic

Build complex rules with if/then logic. Different actions for different scenarios.

Escalation Rules

Automatic escalation when items aren't addressed. Nothing sits waiting for action.

Audit Trail

Full history of workflow execution. See who approved what, when, and why.
