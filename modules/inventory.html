Inventory Control

Complete Visibility & Control

Real-time inventory management across multiple locations. Track lots, manage cycle counts, optimize stock levels, and never lose sight of where your inventory is.

See It In Action View All Modules

Overview

Rover's Inventory Control module gives you complete visibility into your inventory across warehouses, bins, and locations. Whether you need lot tracking for compliance, barcode scanning for accuracy, or intelligent reorder suggestions, Rover has you covered.

Built for growing manufacturers, Rover's intuitive design adapts to your workflows, ensuring fast adoption and long-term scalability. And because it's part of the integrated Rover platform, data flows seamlessly to finance, sales, manufacturing, and every other module.

Capabilities

Key Features

Everything you need for inventory control.

Multi-Location Management

Track inventory across warehouses, zones, bins, and locations. Real-time visibility into what's where.

Lot & Serial Tracking

Full traceability from receipt to shipment. Essential for compliance, recalls, and quality management.

Cycle Counting

Scheduled counts with variance reporting. Maintain accuracy without shutting down for full physical inventory.

Reorder Point Management

Automatic alerts and purchase suggestions when inventory drops below thresholds. Never run out of critical items.

Barcode Integration

Scan-based receiving, picking, transfers, and counts. Eliminate manual data entry errors.

Inventory Valuation

Support for FIFO, LIFO, average, and standard costing methods. Accurate financials, always.

Transfer Management

Move inventory between locations with full visibility. Track in-transit inventory.

Min/Max Planning

Set minimum and maximum stock levels by item and location. Optimize carrying costs.

Inventory Reporting

Real-time dashboards, aging reports, valuation summaries, and movement history.
