Purchasing

Streamlined Procurement

Complete procurement management from requisition to receipt. Manage suppliers, track orders, and optimize your supply chain with integrated purchasing tools.

See It In Action View All Modules

Overview

Rover's Purchasing module streamlines your entire procurement process—from internal requisitions through supplier management to final receipt. With integrated MRP suggestions and supplier performance tracking, you'll make smarter buying decisions.

Built for growing manufacturers, Rover's intuitive design adapts to your workflows, ensuring fast adoption and long-term scalability. And because it's part of the integrated Rover platform, purchasing data flows seamlessly to inventory, finance, and production.

Capabilities

Key Features

Everything you need for procurement operations.

Purchase Orders

Create, approve, and track POs through fulfillment. Multiple approval workflows and change order management.

Supplier Management

Vendor profiles, performance tracking, pricing agreements, and compliance documentation all in one place.

Receiving

Barcode-driven receipt with quality inspection checkpoints. Partial receipts and over/under tolerance handling.

Purchase Requisitions

Internal request workflow with approvals. Convert approved requisitions to POs automatically.

Landed Cost Tracking

Capture freight, duties, customs, and handling costs. Allocate to inventory for true product costing.

Vendor Returns (RMA)

Process returns to vendors with full tracking. Manage credits and replacement shipments.

Blanket Orders

Set up long-term pricing agreements with release scheduling. Lock in prices while maintaining flexibility.

Supplier Scorecards

Track on-time delivery, quality metrics, and pricing compliance. Data-driven supplier decisions.

MRP Integration

Automated purchase suggestions from MRP. Convert recommendations to POs with one click.
