Rover Portal

Self-Service for Customers & Suppliers

Give customers and suppliers secure self-service access to orders, invoices, shipments, and more. Reduce inbound calls while improving satisfaction.

See It In Action View All Modules

Overview

Rover Portal gives your customers and suppliers 24/7 self-service access to the information they need. Customers can track orders, view invoices, make payments, and place new orders. Suppliers can acknowledge POs, submit advance ship notices, and manage their account—all without calling your team.

Built for growing manufacturers, Rover's intuitive design adapts to your workflows, ensuring fast adoption and long-term scalability. And because it's part of the integrated Rover platform, portal data syncs in real-time with your ERP—no manual updates required.

Key Features

Everything you need for portal operations.

Customer Portal

Order status, shipment tracking, invoice viewing, and payment history. Everything customers need in one place.

Self-Service Ordering

Customers place orders directly through the portal. Real-time pricing, availability, and order confirmation.

Invoice Management

View, download, and pay invoices online. Integrated with Rover Pay for instant payment processing.

Supplier Portal

PO acknowledgment, shipment notification, and invoice submission. Streamline supplier collaboration.

Document Access

Customers and suppliers can access relevant documents, specs, drawings, and certifications.

Order Tracking

Real-time visibility into order status from entry through shipment. Tracking numbers and delivery dates.

Custom Branding

Mobile Responsive

Works perfectly on any device. Customers and suppliers can access from anywhere.
