Sales & CRM

Quote to Cash Excellence

Manage customer relationships from first contact to final payment. Track opportunities, process orders, and deliver exceptional customer experiences.

See It In Action View All Modules

Overview

Rover's Sales & CRM module gives your team a complete view of every customer relationship—from prospect to loyal customer. Unlike standalone CRM tools, Rover's sales capabilities connect directly to inventory, pricing, and fulfillment for real-time accuracy.

Built for growing manufacturers, Rover's intuitive design adapts to your workflows, ensuring fast adoption and long-term scalability. Your sales team can check availability, create quotes, and process orders without switching systems.

Capabilities

Key Features

Everything you need for sales success.

Quote Management

Create and track quotes with approval workflows. Convert to orders with one click. Full quote revision history.

Order Entry

Fast order processing with real-time pricing and availability. Configurable product options and kit assembly.

Customer Management

360° view of customer history, orders, invoices, payments, and communications. Everything in one place.

Pricing & Discounts

Customer-specific pricing, volume discounts, promotional pricing, and contract management.

Pipeline Management

Track opportunities through your sales process. Forecasting, probability weighting, and stage management.

Credit Management

Credit limits, automatic holds, and approval workflows. Protect your cash flow without slowing sales.

Sales Reporting

Bookings, pipeline, rep performance, and territory analytics. The metrics that drive growth.

Contact Management

Track contacts, activities, and communication history. Never lose context on a customer relationship.

Commission Tracking

Calculate and track sales commissions by rep, territory, or product. Automated and accurate.
