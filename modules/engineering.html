Engineering

Product Configuration & BOM Management

Powerful product configurator and bill of materials management. Enable sales teams to configure complex products while engineering maintains control.

See It In Action View All Modules

Overview

Rover's Engineering module gives you complete control over product definition—from simple items to complex configured products. Manage multi-level BOMs, routings, and engineering changes with full revision control and approval workflows.

Built for growing manufacturers, Rover's intuitive design adapts to your workflows, ensuring fast adoption and long-term scalability. The product configurator enables sales to build complex quotes while engineering maintains control over valid options.

Capabilities

Key Features

Everything you need for engineering operations.

Bill of Materials

Multi-level BOMs with unlimited depth. Revision control, effectivity dates, and phantom assemblies.

Product Configurator

Rules-based configuration for complex products. Valid option enforcement ensures accurate quotes every time.

Routing Management

Define operations, work centers, and labor standards. Outside processing and alternate routings supported.

ECO Management

Engineering change orders with approval workflow. Track changes, affected items, and implementation status.

Where-Used Analysis

Instantly see where any component is used across all products. Essential for change impact analysis.

Document Management

Attach drawings, specifications, and work instructions to items, BOMs, and operations.

Revision Control

Full revision history with effectivity dates. See what changed, when, and why.

Cost Rollup

Automatic cost calculation based on BOM and routing. Material, labor, and overhead all included.

CAD Integration

Import BOMs from CAD systems. Keep engineering and ERP in sync automatically.

Why It Matters

Benefits for Your Business

  • Maintain engineering control with approval workflows and valid option rules
  • Accurate costing with detailed BOMs and routings for better pricing decisions
  • Full revision history and change tracking for compliance and quality
  • Reduce errors with enforced configuration rules and validation
