OB3 for Manufacturers – Quick-Check Guide

The manufacturers who gain the most from OB3 will be the ones who track and categorize investments accurately. Use this quick-start guide to calculate your potential first year OB3 cash-flow boost.

The Oregon Business & Innovation Incentives (OB3) program — and similar R&D tax incentive programs at the state and federal level — represent a significant, often overlooked source of cash-flow improvement for manufacturers. The manufacturers who capture the most value aren't necessarily the ones doing the most innovative work. They're the ones tracking and categorizing their qualifying activities and expenditures most carefully.

This quick-check guide walks you through the key categories, the calculation framework, and how to use your ERP data to build a defensible, accurate claim.

What Is OB3?

Oregon's OB3 (Oregon Business & Innovation Incentives) program provides tax credits for qualifying research and development activities conducted in Oregon. Manufacturers in qualifying sectors — including advanced manufacturing, electronics, and industrial technology — may be eligible for credits ranging from 5% to 25% of qualifying expenditures.

Step 1: Identify Your Qualifying Activities

The first step is identifying which of your operations qualify as R&D activities under the program's definitions. Common qualifying activities in manufacturing include:

  • New product development: Engineering hours, prototyping costs, and materials consumed in developing new products
  • Process improvement projects: Labor and materials used to develop improved manufacturing processes — not routine maintenance, but genuine process innovation
  • Software development: Internal development of software used in manufacturing operations, quality control, or process automation
  • Testing and evaluation: Costs of testing new products or processes, including materials consumed and labor time
  • Tooling and fixture development: Custom tooling or fixtures designed for new products or processes (not standard, catalog items)
  • Technical uncertainty resolution: Work done specifically to resolve technical uncertainty — if you knew it would work from the start, it likely doesn't qualify

Step 2: Calculate Your Qualifying Expenditures

For each qualifying activity, you'll need to calculate three categories of expenditures:

Wages & Labor

The largest component for most manufacturers. Qualifying wages include time spent directly on qualifying R&D activities by employees in the following roles:

  • Engineers and product developers
  • Manufacturing technicians working on qualifying projects
  • Quality engineers involved in testing and evaluation
  • Software developers building qualifying internal tools
  • Supervisors who directly oversee qualifying activities (a portion of their time)

Your ERP's labor reporting and time tracking data is your primary source here. Rover ERP's project tracking and work order labor reports can extract this data by project, department, and employee.

Supplies & Materials

Materials consumed during qualifying R&D activities — not sold to customers, but used in the experimental process. This includes:

  • Prototype materials
  • Test samples destroyed during evaluation
  • Materials consumed in failed attempts (these count too)
  • Supplies used exclusively for qualifying testing activities

Rover ERP's project cost tracking and material issue reports can isolate these expenditures by project or work order.

Contract Research

Payments to third parties for qualifying research activities conducted on your behalf. Up to 65% of qualifying contract research expenditures may be included. Keep your vendor invoices and contracts — documentation requirements are strict.

5–25% Tax credit rate on qualifying OB3 expenditures
65% Of contract research expenditures that may qualify
3 years Lookback window for amended returns in most cases

Step 3: Use Your ERP Data

The quality of your OB3 claim depends heavily on the quality of your documentation — and that documentation lives in your ERP system. Here's how to use Rover ERP to build a strong, defensible record:

Project Costing

If you're using Rover ERP's project module, you can tag projects as R&D and pull complete cost summaries including labor hours by employee, material costs by item, and any contract or outside service costs. This produces an audit-ready cost report that maps directly to the OB3 calculation worksheets.

Work Order Tracking

For manufacturers who use work orders for development and prototyping work, Rover ERP's work order reports can extract actual labor and material costs for any set of work orders over a specified time period. Filter by work order type or description to isolate qualifying activity.

Time Allocation

Engineers and technicians who split time between qualifying R&D and routine production need a way to document that allocation. Even rough percentages — documented contemporaneously — are significantly more defensible than estimates made retrospectively.

Step 4: Estimate Your First-Year Benefit

Use this simplified formula to estimate your potential benefit:

Qualifying wages × credit rate + qualifying supplies × credit rate + qualifying contract research × 65% × credit rate = estimated annual credit

For most manufacturers actively engaged in product development or process improvement, the qualifying expenditures often reach into the hundreds of thousands of dollars — generating credits that can meaningfully improve annual cash flow.

Getting Started

The best time to start tracking qualifying expenditures is before you need to file the claim — not after. Building project codes, work order types, and labor categories that align with OB3 qualifying activity definitions into your ERP now will make every future filing much simpler.
