Rover ERP Master Class – Purchase to Payment

In this month's Rover ERP Master Class, we focused on the Purchase-to-Payment (P2P) process — helping teams streamline the journey from vendor request to final payment.

Purchase-to-Payment is one of the highest-volume processes in any manufacturing or distribution business — and one of the most prone to errors, delays, and cost leakage when it's not properly controlled. In September's Master Class, we walked through the complete P2P cycle in Rover ERP, demonstrating how each step connects to reduce manual work, improve visibility, and tighten financial controls.

What We Covered

The complete Purchase-to-Payment cycle: Requisition and PO Creation, Receiving and Three-Way Match, AP Invoice Processing, and Payment and Vendor Management.

Step 1: Requisition and PO Creation

The P2P process starts before the purchase order — with the requisition. Rover ERP's requisition workflow allows any authorized user to request a purchase without having direct PO creation access.

Requisition Workflow

  • Requestors submit purchase requisitions specifying the item, quantity, required date, and suggested vendor
  • Requisitions route through a configurable approval chain based on dollar value and category
  • Approved requisitions convert to purchase orders with one click — no re-entry of data
  • Requestors receive automatic notification when their requisition is approved, rejected, or converted

Purchase Order Entry

For direct PO creation, Rover ERP's PO entry screen includes several efficiency features demonstrated in the session:

  • Vendor catalog integration: Select items from the vendor's price book — pricing, units, and lead times pull automatically
  • Last purchase history: View the last price paid for an item from this vendor inline on the PO line
  • MRP-suggested quantities: If the PO is being created to fill an MRP requirement, the suggested quantity and required date are pre-populated
  • Blanket PO support: Create blanket orders with scheduled release dates for recurring purchases

Step 2: Receiving and Three-Way Match

Receiving is where the physical and financial worlds meet. Rover ERP's receiving screen connects what was ordered, what arrived, and what will be invoiced — the foundation of three-way match.

PO Receipt Process

  • Select the open PO from the receiving screen — all open lines display with ordered quantities and any previously received amounts
  • Enter or scan received quantities for each line — partial receipts are fully supported
  • Record the vendor's packing slip number for audit trail purposes
  • Lot and serial number assignment at time of receipt for tracked items
  • Automatic inventory update on save — goods are available in the system immediately

Three-Way Match Setup

Rover ERP's three-way match compares the PO, the receipt, and the vendor invoice automatically. The demo showed the three tolerance configurations organizations typically use:

  • Strict match: Invoice must match PO price and received quantity exactly — any variance holds the invoice for review
  • Tolerance-based: Allow price variances up to a configured percentage (e.g., 2%) and quantity variances up to a configured amount before triggering a review hold
  • Quantity-only match: Match on received quantity, accepting any price as long as it was on an approved PO

Step 3: AP Invoice Processing

AP invoice processing is typically where the P2P process slows down — invoices sitting in approval queues, questions about PO references, discrepancies holding up payment. The demo showed how Rover ERP streamlines this.

Invoice Entry and Matching

  • Enter the vendor invoice — or import it from EDI or email capture
  • Link to the associated PO and receipt — the system performs three-way match automatically
  • Matched invoices route directly to the payment queue
  • Mismatched invoices are flagged with the specific variance (price, quantity, or both) and routed to the appropriate reviewer

Invoice Approval

Invoices that pass three-way match can be auto-approved up to configured thresholds. Those that require human review route through Rover ERP's approval workflow — with email notification, deadline tracking, and escalation for approvals that stall.

Step 4: Payment Processing

  • The AP aging dashboard shows all approved invoices by due date, vendor, and payment priority
  • Payment run selection: Choose invoices to pay by due date, vendor, or manually — or let the system select all invoices due within a specified date range
  • Early payment discount capture: The system flags invoices where taking an early payment discount is financially beneficial
  • Payment method by vendor: ACH, check, wire, or Rover Pay — configured per vendor
  • Post-payment audit trail: Every payment is linked back to the invoice, receipt, and PO for complete traceability
Automatic Three-way match on every AP invoice
Zero Re-entry of data from requisition to PO to receipt
Complete Audit trail from PO through final payment
