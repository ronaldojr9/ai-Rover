Rover ERP Master Class – Boosting Support Efficiency with Rover ERP

In this month's Rover ERP Master Class, we focus on one of the most exciting enhancements we've introduced: The Rover AI Technical Support Assistant.

Customer support teams at manufacturers and distributors deal with a constant stream of requests: order status checks, invoice questions, shipping inquiries, product specifications. These interactions take time — time that could be spent on higher-value work. In December's Master Class, we showed how the Rover AI Technical Support Assistant transforms this dynamic.

What We Covered

This session demonstrated the Rover AI Technical Support Assistant — how it's configured, how it connects to live ERP data, and how support teams are using it to resolve customer inquiries faster than ever before.

The Rover AI Technical Support Assistant

The Rover AI Technical Support Assistant is an AI-powered tool that gives your support staff (and optionally your customers) instant access to answers from your live ERP data — without having to run reports, search screens, or wait on someone with system access.

Ask it a question in plain English. Get a direct answer, with supporting data from Rover ERP.

Example Queries It Can Answer

  • "What's the current status of order #45821 for Acme Manufacturing?"
  • "When is the next shipment of part #BRK-1044 expected to arrive?"
  • "What invoices are open for customer ID 10234?"
  • "How many units of PN-8823 do we have in stock at the Phoenix warehouse?"
  • "What's the lead time for vendor V-0042 on steel components?"
  • "Has the RMA for return authorization RA-7710 been processed?"

The assistant answers these in seconds — pulling from live Rover ERP data, not a snapshot or a report.

How It Works

Connected to Live ERP Data

The assistant connects directly to your Rover ERP environment through the Rover Data Services layer. When a question is asked, the assistant:

  1. Interprets the question using natural language understanding
  2. Determines which ERP tables and fields are relevant
  3. Executes a secure, read-only query against your live data
  4. Formats the result as a plain-language answer

No data is stored outside your environment. The assistant accesses only the data your Rover security policies allow.

Role-Based Access Control

The assistant respects Rover ERP's existing security model. Configure which data the assistant can access based on the role of the person asking — customer-facing portals see only their own order data, while internal support staff can access the full customer record.

Deployment Options

Internal Support Tool

Deploy the assistant for your internal support team as a chat interface within Rover Web. Support staff get answers instantly without navigating menus or running reports. Average handle time for common inquiries drops significantly — in early customer deployments, teams report handling 2–3× more inquiries per hour with the same staff.

Customer-Facing Portal Integration

The assistant can be embedded directly into the Rover Customer Portal, giving your customers self-service access to their own order data. Customers check order status, download invoices, and get shipping updates without calling or emailing your team.

2–3× More inquiries handled per hour vs. manual lookup
<3 sec Average response time for common ERP queries
24/7 Available for customer self-service in the portal

Configuration & Setup

Setting up the Rover AI Technical Support Assistant requires configuration in the Rover AI administration panel:

  • Define which data entities the assistant can access (orders, invoices, inventory, etc.)
  • Set security boundaries by user role or customer account
  • Configure the assistant's persona and response style for customer-facing deployments
  • Test queries and review the assistant's responses before going live
  • Enable logging for audit and quality review purposes
