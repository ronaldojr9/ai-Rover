Rover ERP December 2024 Release Notice

The December 2024 release delivers the Rover AI Assistant to Enterprise subscribers, enhanced inventory lookup tables, custom POS subtotaling, and a redesigned Messages & Alerts experience.

The December 2024 release — available now for all Rover ERP customers — brings significant enhancements across multiple areas of the platform. From the debut of the Rover AI Assistant for Enterprise subscribers to improved inventory tooling, POS transparency improvements, and a fully revamped messaging experience in the .NET client, this release reflects months of feedback-driven development from our manufacturing and distribution customers.

★ Rover AI Assistant

The headline feature of this release is the Rover AI Assistant, now available for Enterprise subscribers who utilize Rover BI. This is not a generic chatbot — it is an end-to-end ERP assistant deeply integrated with your Rover ERP data and workflows.

What Makes the Rover AI Assistant Different

Most "AI" features in ERP systems are bolt-ons that can answer questions but can't take action. Rover AI can both answer questions about your business data and take action — creating purchase orders, updating records, and generating reports — all from a single natural language interface.

With the Rover AI Assistant, you can:

  • Ask questions the way you'd ask a colleague: "What are our current inventory levels for part XYZ?" or "How many open orders do we have for Acme Corp?"
  • Get instant answers about inventory levels, open orders, and production status — without navigating through multiple screens
  • Take direct action: create purchase orders, update records, and generate reports through conversational prompts
  • Work faster without requiring deep ERP knowledge from every user on your team

The assistant is built on Zumasys's AI platform and is deeply integrated with Rover ERP data — meaning answers reflect your actual live business data, not generic examples. Enterprise subscribers with Rover BI can enable the assistant through their account settings.

Inventory Enhancements

Custom Part Inventory Lookup tables received a meaningful upgrade in this release. Users can now sort and filter lookup results — two capabilities that were among the most frequently requested by customers managing complex, multi-warehouse environments.

  • Sort columns ascending or descending by any column header
  • Filter results by warehouse, location, or part status
  • Faster part discovery without scrolling through hundreds of rows

For operations with dozens of warehouses or thousands of SKUs, these improvements significantly reduce the time it takes to locate the right part at the right location.

POS Cart Updates

The POS cart summary view now supports custom subtotaling. Integration-specific subtotal line items — such as freight charges, handling fees, and taxes — can now be displayed directly in the cart view.

This gives customers reviewing their orders a clearer picture of the full cost before confirmation, and gives your team a more accurate summary during order entry. Subtotal items are configured at the integration level, so the display adapts to your specific business rules.

Messages & Alerts (.NET Client)

Messages and Alerts are now fully functional in the .NET client, with an entirely redesigned experience built around how modern manufacturing teams actually work:

  • New Messages pane — a dedicated inbox for incoming ERP notifications, organized and easy to action
  • New Tasks pane — direct interaction with task-based workflows; see what requires your attention and act on it without leaving the pane
  • Windows toast alerts — Alerts now surface as native Windows toast notifications, so you'll see them even when you're not actively in the ERP screen

Never Miss a Critical Alert

With toast notifications, critical alerts — like an inventory drop below reorder point or an overdue work order — surface at the OS level. Your team stays informed without needing to monitor a screen.

Opportunities Table Improvements

The Opportunities table has been updated with two performance and usability improvements:

  • Batch fetching — results are now fetched in smaller batches, significantly improving performance on large opportunity datasets with hundreds or thousands of records
  • Inline opportunity creation — users can now create a new opportunity directly from within Customer Inquiry without leaving the current customer view; the new opportunity is immediately reflected in the opportunities table

These changes benefit sales teams who work with large pipelines and need to log new opportunities quickly during a customer call or meeting.

How to Get This Release

The December 2024 release is available now for all Rover ERP customers. Cloud-hosted customers have already received the update automatically. On-premise customers should contact Zumasys support to schedule their upgrade. Enterprise subscribers can enable the Rover AI Assistant through their account settings once the update is applied.
