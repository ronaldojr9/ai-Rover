Rover ERP Demo Day Recap: Interface Updates, Reporting & What's Coming Next

Our March Demo Day showcased the latest improvements to Rover's interface and reporting tools, plus an exciting sneak peek at capabilities on the product roadmap.

What Is Rover Demo Day?

Rover's quarterly Demo Day brings together the product team and our customer community to do two things: show what we have shipped, and listen to what you need next. It is one of the most direct feedback loops we have as a product organization, and we take it seriously.

March Demo Day drew strong participation from manufacturers and distributors across the Rover customer base, with attendees ranging from production managers and IT administrators to operations directors and CFOs. The session ran approximately 90 minutes and covered four major topic areas: interface improvements, reporting enhancements, inventory management updates, and a preview of upcoming AI integration.

Interface Improvements

The Rover Web interface received a meaningful round of refinements targeting day-to-day usability. Navigation has been reorganized based on workflow analysis of how customers actually move through the system, reducing the number of clicks required for common tasks. Page load times have been reduced across the most frequently accessed screens, with particular improvement in inventory inquiry and order management views.

Mobile responsiveness in Rover Web has also been improved significantly. For customers who access the system from tablets on the shop floor or from phones while traveling, the updated layouts adapt more gracefully to smaller screens without sacrificing access to critical data fields. Form inputs, grid views, and action buttons have all been reconfigured for touch interaction where applicable.

Reporting Enhancements

The reporting toolset received one of the largest upgrades in this release cycle. Key improvements include:

  • New drag-and-drop report builder. Users can now construct custom reports by selecting and reordering columns visually, without requiring technical configuration. The report builder supports filters, grouping, sorting, and calculated fields through a no-code interface.
  • Saved report configurations. Reports built in the new builder can be saved, named, and shared with other users — eliminating the need to reconstruct commonly used report layouts from scratch each time.
  • Improved export options. Reports can now be exported to PDF, Excel, and CSV formats directly from the report viewer, with formatting preserved in the PDF output. Scheduled exports and email delivery are on the roadmap for a future release.

Inventory Management Updates

Several targeted improvements were made to inventory management workflows based on customer feedback from the previous quarter:

  • Bin location management improvements. Bin assignment and transfer workflows have been streamlined, and the bin location inquiry now shows real-time quantity and reservation data without requiring a separate inquiry step.
  • Enhanced cycle count workflows. The cycle count process has been redesigned to support zone-based counting, blind count mode (where expected quantities are hidden from counters to eliminate bias), and automatic variance flagging when count results exceed configurable tolerance thresholds.
  • Better lot and serial tracking visibility. Lot and serial number history is now accessible from more entry points in the system, and the traceability view has been expanded to show the full chain of custody from receipt through shipment in a single screen.
AI Integration Preview

One of the most-discussed segments of Demo Day was a preview of Rover AI integration within the ERP workflow. The demo showed Rover AI surfacing reorder recommendations based on current inventory levels and historical usage patterns, answering natural language questions about order status and production schedules, and flagging anomalies in purchasing data that warranted review. Full Rover AI integration is available in the Enterprise tier and will be expanded in upcoming releases.

What Is Coming Next: Roadmap Preview

The product team shared a forward-looking preview of capabilities currently in development. While release timing is subject to change, the following items are actively in progress:

  • Expanded Rover BI dashboards. New pre-built dashboard templates for production performance, inventory turns, and customer order metrics — designed to be usable out of the box without custom configuration.
  • Enhanced customer portal. An updated customer-facing portal that allows end customers to check order status, view shipment history, and submit inquiries without contacting the sales team directly.
  • New EDI capabilities. Expanded EDI transaction support and improved trading partner onboarding for manufacturers working with customers and suppliers who require electronic data interchange.

How Rover Builds Its Roadmap

Every item on the roadmap preview shown at Demo Day traces directly back to customer feedback. The product team reviews support tickets, attends customer calls, and analyzes usage data to identify where the system creates friction and where new capabilities would deliver the most value.

A sincere thank-you to every customer who participated in March Demo Day — your input shapes what we build next. If you were unable to attend, the session recording is available in the Rover customer portal.

How to Attend Future Demo Days

Rover Demo Day is available to all Rover ERP customers at no additional cost. Sessions are held quarterly and cover the most recent product updates alongside a look ahead at upcoming capabilities. To register for the next Demo Day, contact your Rover account manager or check the Events section of the Rover customer portal. Attendance is open to all users at your organization — we encourage you to bring the people who use the system every day, not just administrators and managers.
