Meet Tyler Hansen: Bringing Real-World ERP and AI Experience to Rover ERP

As Rover ERP's newest Implementation Engineer with customer and consulting side experience, Tyler sees things differently — making him a powerful addition to our team.

Most implementation engineers come from one side of the table: they've either worked at software companies, or they've worked as the customer using that software. Tyler Hansen has done both — and that combination of perspectives is exactly what makes him such a strong addition to the Rover ERP team.

Both Sides of the Table

Before joining Zumasys, Tyler spent years working in manufacturing and distribution operations — not in IT, but on the floor and in the office, using ERP systems to manage purchasing, inventory, and production planning. He knows what it feels like when the system slows you down, when you can't find the data you need, or when a workflow doesn't match the way your business actually operates.

From there, Tyler moved into ERP consulting, helping manufacturers and distributors implement and optimize their systems. He's sat with operations teams, finance teams, and warehouse managers trying to figure out how to make their ERP work better for them — not just technically correct, but genuinely useful.

"Most ERP consultants understand the software really well," Tyler says. "I've been the person on the other side of that conversation — the one who has to live with the system every day. That changes how you approach an implementation."

Why Rover ERP

Tyler joined Zumasys because of what he sees as a genuine alignment between Rover ERP's direction and what manufacturing and distribution businesses actually need in 2025 and beyond.

"A lot of ERP systems are still built around the assumption that you'll have a dedicated IT team managing everything," he says. "Rover is going a different direction — bringing AI and modern web tools to the platform so that the business users can get more out of the system without needing to call IT for everything. That's where the industry needs to go."

He's particularly excited about the Rover AI capabilities. Having worked with AI tools in his previous consulting role, he understands both the potential and the practical challenges of connecting AI to real business data. "The value isn't in the AI itself — it's in what you can do with it when it's connected to your actual ERP data. That's what Rover is building."

What He's Working On

As an Implementation Engineer, Tyler works directly with Rover ERP customers to help them get the most out of the platform. That includes onboarding new customers, running training sessions, configuring workflows and approvals, and helping teams adopt the features that will have the biggest impact on their operations.

He's already leading several of the Rover Master Class sessions — including the recent sessions on Rover BI and the AI Technical Support Assistant — bringing his real-world perspective to each walkthrough.

Outside of Work

When he's not working with Rover ERP customers, Tyler is an avid outdoorsman — hiking, fishing, and spending time in the mountains whenever he gets the chance. He also keeps up with developments in AI and machine learning, viewing them not just as professional tools but as genuinely fascinating technology.

"We're at this moment where AI is moving from experimental to practical — where real businesses are getting real value from it," he says. "Being at a company that's in the middle of that transition, for an industry I understand well, is a pretty great place to be."

Connect with Tyler

If you're a Rover ERP customer looking to get more from the platform — whether it's production workflows, AI tools, or BI reporting — reach out through the Rover support portal or ask your account manager to connect you with Tyler directly.
