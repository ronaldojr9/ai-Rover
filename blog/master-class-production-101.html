Rover ERP Master Class – Production 101: New Features & Enhancements

Our May Master Class spotlighted powerful new production tools giving teams more flexibility, insight, and control on the shop floor.

Building the Production Tools Manufacturers Actually Need

Rover ERP's May Master Class focused entirely on production — and for good reason. Feedback from customers across job shop manufacturing, contract production, and mixed-mode environments made clear that scheduling visibility, conflict resolution, and shop floor control were the capabilities our users needed most.

Over the past development cycle, the Rover product team delivered a significant round of production enhancements in direct response to that input. The Master Class was the opportunity to walk customers through what is new, what has changed, and how to put these capabilities to work in their operations immediately.

Here is a full summary of the eight major enhancements covered in the session.

Feature 1: Visual Work Order Status Indicators

Sub-statuses on work orders now drive color indicators directly on the Gantt chart. At a glance, planners and supervisors can distinguish between jobs that are on track, at risk, on hold, or awaiting material — without opening individual records. This visual layer of information is particularly valuable on busy shop floors where planners are managing dozens of simultaneous work orders and need to identify exceptions quickly.

Feature 2: Scheduling Logic Enforcement

The scheduling engine now validates work center assignments during the scheduling process and enforces valid work centers before a schedule is committed. Previously, an incorrect work center assignment could propagate through the schedule and only surface as a problem during execution. The new enforcement logic catches these errors upstream, preventing them from cascading into production delays.

Feature 3: Auto-Push Conflict Resolution

When a scheduling conflict is detected — for example, when a new job is inserted into a schedule that is already at capacity — the auto-push feature automatically adjusts downstream operations to resolve the conflict. This eliminates significant manual effort for production planners, who previously had to recalculate and reschedule affected operations by hand. For shops with complex, interdependent schedules, auto-push can save hours of planning time per week.

Feature 4: Production Board View

The new Production Board is a purpose-built dashboard giving supervisors and production managers real-time visibility into all active work orders, organized by work center. The board surfaces job status, sub-status color indicators, and scheduling information in a format optimized for the shop floor — designed to be readable at a distance on a monitor mounted in a production area, or reviewed quickly on a tablet during a floor walk.

What the Production Board replaces:

For most shops, the Production Board replaces a combination of whiteboards, printed schedules, and status update meetings. When all of that information lives in a single real-time view, the cost of staying aligned drops dramatically — and the time supervisors spend gathering status information is redirected toward solving problems instead.

Feature 5: Enhanced Routing Control

Rover ERP now supports alternate routings per part number, giving planners the flexibility to route production through alternate work centers when a primary work center is unavailable, overloaded, or undergoing maintenance. This capability is particularly valuable for shops that have invested in redundant capacity for critical operations, and for custom work where the optimal routing depends on current floor conditions.

Feature 6: Work Order Split

Large work orders can now be split into smaller, more manageable units directly within Rover ERP. This capability supports partial shipment scenarios, enables better load balancing across work centers, and gives planners the flexibility to prioritize a portion of a large order without waiting for the entire quantity to complete. Split work orders maintain full traceability back to the original order and customer requirements.

Feature 7: Enhanced Work Order Inquiry (WOQ)

The Work Order Inquiry screen has been significantly enhanced to provide quick, actionable insights into the state of scheduled and unscheduled operations across all active work orders. Planners can now identify scheduling gaps — operations that exist on work orders but have not yet been scheduled — and take corrective action before those gaps become production delays. The enhanced WOQ is a practical daily tool for any planner managing a complex production environment.

Feature 8: Full CRP Support in Rover Web

Complete Capacity Requirements Planning (CRP) is now fully supported in Rover Web, bringing one of manufacturing ERP's most powerful planning tools to the modern interface. CRP allows planners to compare projected load against available capacity across all work centers over a planning horizon, identify bottlenecks before they occur, and make proactive adjustments to schedules, staffing, and capacity. For growing manufacturers, CRP is often the capability that moves production planning from reactive to strategic.

How to Access These Features

All eight capabilities described above are available in the current release of Rover Web. Some features may require configuration by your Rover administrator or support team to enable fully. Contact your Rover support representative for a walkthrough of the Production Board, CRP setup, and alternate routing configuration specific to your environment. Recordings of the Master Class session are available to all Rover ERP customers through the customer portal.
