Rover ERP Master Class – From Quote to Cash with Rover ERP

In this month's Rover ERP Master Class, we walk through one of the most critical processes for manufacturers and distributors: Quote to Cash — from the moment a customer request comes in to final payment.

Quote to Cash is the revenue engine of every manufacturing and distribution business. It's the process that turns a customer's need into money in the bank — and every delay, error, or miscommunication along the way costs real money. In August's Master Class, we walked through the complete Quote to Cash cycle in Rover ERP, showing how each step connects to eliminate friction and accelerate cash flow.

What We Covered

The complete Quote-to-Cash cycle: Quote Creation and Pricing, Order Entry and Confirmation, Fulfillment and Shipping, and Invoicing and AR Collection — demonstrated in both the Rover Web interface and the .NET desktop client.

Step 1: Quote Creation and Pricing

Every Q2C cycle starts with a customer request. Rover ERP's quoting tools are designed for speed and accuracy — giving sales reps everything they need to put together a competitive, profitable quote without having to track down information from other systems or people.

Quote Entry

  • Customer lookup pulls contact, terms, pricing tier, and credit status automatically
  • Part search with live inventory visibility — sales reps can see available quantities before quoting a delivery date
  • Automatic pricing based on the customer's assigned price list, quantity breaks, or contract pricing
  • Margin visibility on every line — see the gross margin impact of any discount before it's offered
  • Quote validity date: Set expiration dates on quotes — expired quotes can't be converted without review

Pricing Tools

In the session, we demonstrated Rover ERP's pricing engine in detail:

  • Price lists: Multiple price lists by customer tier, market segment, or sales channel
  • Contract pricing: Item- and customer-specific prices that override standard price lists
  • Quantity breaks: Automatic price reductions for larger order quantities
  • Cost-plus pricing: For make-to-order items, price based on estimated cost plus a configured margin
  • Override controls: Discount limits by user role — sales reps can offer up to 5%, managers up to 15%, VP approval required above that

Step 2: Order Entry and Confirmation

When a customer accepts a quote, converting it to a sales order in Rover ERP is a single action — all quote data flows to the order with no re-entry.

Quote-to-Order Conversion

  • Click "Convert to Order" on any approved quote — the sales order is created instantly
  • All line items, pricing, terms, ship-to address, and notes carry forward
  • Attachments and documents on the quote are linked to the order automatically
  • The customer receives an order confirmation email — configurable per customer as to format and content

Credit and Compliance Checks

On order entry, Rover ERP automatically checks:

  • Customer credit limit — orders that would push the customer over limit are flagged for approval
  • Past due balance — customers with significant past due balances can be put on order hold pending payment
  • Tax exemption status — orders for tax-exempt customers calculate $0 tax automatically
  • Export compliance — for international orders, flags items that require export license verification

Step 3: Fulfillment and Shipping

Order fulfillment in Rover ERP connects the sales order directly to your warehouse operations — pick tickets, shipping documentation, and carrier integration all flow from the single order record.

Pick and Ship Workflow

  • Pick tickets generated automatically when orders are released to the warehouse
  • Pick assignment by warehouse zone, picker, or wave — configurable for your operation
  • Scan-to-confirm picking in Rover Web — no separate WMS needed for standard operations
  • Packing list and shipping label generation on pack completion
  • Carrier integration for rate shopping and automated tracking number capture
  • Customer shipment notification sent automatically with tracking information

Step 4: Invoicing and AR Collection

The final step converts the fulfilled order into revenue and starts the collection cycle.

Invoice Generation

  • Invoices can be generated automatically on shipment or held for batch processing — configurable per customer or customer group
  • Invoice content reflects actual shipped quantities — partial shipments invoice for what shipped, not what was ordered
  • Electronic delivery: Email, EDI, or customer portal — configured per customer preference
  • Invoice copies attached to the order record for full traceability

AR Collection

  • AR aging dashboard shows all open invoices by customer and days outstanding
  • Automated payment reminders at configured intervals (30 days, 60 days, 90 days)
  • Rover Pay integration: Customers can pay invoices directly from the portal by credit card or ACH
  • Cash application: Payments apply to invoices automatically when customer payment references match — exception queue for unmatched payments
