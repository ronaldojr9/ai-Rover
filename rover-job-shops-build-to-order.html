The Job Shop Challenge

When Every Order Is a One-Off

Job shops thrive on variety—but that variety creates chaos when you're tracking dozens of unique jobs, each with different materials, routings, and delivery dates. Without tight control, margins erode and deadlines slip.

📝

Quoting Complexity

Every RFQ requires a unique estimate—materials, outside processes, setup time, and run time. Slow quotes lose jobs; inaccurate quotes lose money.

💸

Job Cost Tracking

Knowing your actual costs per job—not just your estimates—is the difference between profit and loss. Most shops don't know until it's too late.

🔥

Scheduling Chaos

New rush jobs constantly disrupting the schedule. Without dynamic scheduling tools, the shop floor is a constant game of firefighting.

🧠

Tribal Knowledge

Routings, setup notes, and customer preferences live in people's heads. When someone's absent, institutional knowledge walks out the door.

How Rover Solves It

Quote It. Build It. Ship It. Know Your Costs.

01

Rapid Quoting & Estimating

Build quotes from templates or from scratch with real-time material pricing, shop rates, and outside processing costs. Clone similar past jobs to accelerate new quotes.

02

Job Costing & Tracking

Track actual material, labor, and overhead against estimates in real-time. See margin erosion the moment it starts, not after the job ships.

Every job is a profit center with real-time visibility into actual vs. estimated performance.

03

Dynamic Scheduling

Drag-and-drop scheduling with finite capacity awareness. Insert rush jobs without blowing up the rest of the schedule.

Give your customers realistic delivery dates and your shop floor an achievable plan.

Routing & Work Instructions

Capture routings, setup instructions, and customer-specific notes in the system. Every operator has access to complete job requirements at their station.

Turn tribal knowledge into institutional knowledge that's always available.

05

Outside Processing Management

Track work sent to subcontractors with PO integration, due dates, and receiving workflows. Never lose track of material at a vendor.

Manage your extended value chain as seamlessly as your internal operations.

06

Customer Portal & Communication

Give customers real-time visibility into order status. Automated notifications for job milestones reduce phone calls and build trust.

Spend less time answering 'where's my order?' and more time running your shop.
