Our Proven Framework

A High-Level Roadmap from Kickoff to Go-Live

This timeline outlines what is involved in the process and can be adjusted to match your team's readiness.

Request a Detailed Implementation Plan →

1

Discovery, Goals & BUILD™ Framework

Weeks 1-2

We align on business outcomes first, then use our BUILD™ framework to define scope, owners, milestones, and success criteria for each phase.

2

Data, Roles & Core ERP Setup

Weeks 3-5

We prepare master data, user roles, and permissions so your team can start with reliable ERP foundations across inventory, purchasing, production, and finance.

3

Process Mapping & SOP Alignment

Weeks 6-8

We map current workflows and document SOPs so teams know exactly how work should move through Rover from quote to cash and procure to pay.

4

Training, Enablement & AI Support

Weeks 9-10

We combine role-based training with AI-assisted help content, guided support, and practical coaching so users can learn in the flow of work.

5

Readiness, Validation & Cutover Planning

Weeks 11-12

Before launch, we validate real operating scenarios, confirm data integrity, and finalize a go-live plan that fits your calendar and risk tolerance.

🚀

Go-Live & Continuous Improvement

Go-Live Week

At go-live, our team stays close to monitor adoption, resolve issues quickly, and plan next-phase modules once the core ERP foundation is stable.
