How It Works

From Invoice to Cash—Automatically

  • 1

    Invoice Goes Out

    Create an invoice in your system. Rover Pay automatically attaches a secure payment link.

  • 2

    Customer Pays

    Your customer clicks the link and pays via card, ACH, or their preferred method. No friction.

  • 3

    Auto-Reconciliation

    Payment is automatically matched to the invoice. AR is updated instantly. No manual work.

  • 4

    Cash in Bank

    Funds settle to your account. Full visibility from order to cash, every step of the way.
