The Wire Harness Challenge

When Every Pin, Terminal, and Crimp Matters

Wire harness manufacturing sits at the intersection of precision and volume. Thousands of connectors, terminals, seals, and wire types combine into assemblies that must meet exacting OEM specifications—and one wrong crimp can ground an entire production line.

🔌

Assembly Complexity

A single harness can contain hundreds of wires, dozens of connectors, and multiple splice points—each with specific crimp specifications and routing requirements.

📝

Engineering Change Overload

OEM customers send frequent ECOs that cascade through BOMs, work instructions, and active production orders. Managing changes manually invites errors.

🗂️

Component Proliferation

Terminals, housings, seals, and clips from dozens of suppliers—each with cross-references, approved alternatives, and minimum order quantities.

🏁

OEM Compliance & PPAP

Automotive OEMs demand PPAP documentation, IMDS reporting, and full traceability. Missing any requirement risks losing the program.
