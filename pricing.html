FAQ

Common Questions

Can I start with just one product?

Absolutely. Most customers start with Rover AI to get quick wins, then expand to Pay and ERP as needs grow. Each product works standalone.

What's the minimum commitment?

Rover ERP requires a 5-user minimum. Rover AI and Pay have no minimum user requirements—just the monthly subscription.

Are there setup fees?

Implementation is quoted separately based on your specific needs. We'll provide a clear scope and fixed price before any work begins.

Can I try before I buy?

We offer personalized demos where we can connect to your actual data and show you real insights. Schedule a demo to see Rover in action.

What support is included?

All plans include support. Standard plans get business hours support; higher tiers get priority support and dedicated success managers.

How does volume pricing work for Rover Pay?

For businesses processing over $100K/month, we offer custom rates. Contact our team to discuss volume discounts.
