The Discrete Manufacturing Challenge

When You're Building Distinct Products at Scale

Discrete manufacturers make countable, tangible products—from metal brackets to electronic assemblies. The challenge isn't just making them; it's managing the hundreds of moving pieces that go into getting the right products out the door on time and under budget.

⚙️

Production Complexity

Multiple product lines, each with unique BOMs, routings, and quality requirements. Scheduling across shared resources without visibility leads to bottlenecks.

📦

Inventory Control

Raw materials, WIP, and finished goods across multiple locations. Without real-time visibility, you're either overstocked or expediting emergency purchases.

✅

Quality Management

Every part needs inspection, every process needs documentation. Manual quality tracking creates gaps that surface during audits or—worse—at the customer.

💰

Cost Visibility

Actual vs. standard cost variances hide in spreadsheets. Without real-time cost tracking, you find out you lost money on a job after it's already shipped.

How Rover Solves It

Full Production Control—From Order to Shipment

01

Work Order Management

Create, release, and track work orders with real-time status updates. See where every job stands across every work center without walking the floor.

Eliminate the production whiteboard and give everyone—from operators to executives—the same real-time picture.

Bill of Materials

Multi-level BOMs with revision control, effectivity dates, and phantom assemblies. Engineering changes propagate automatically to all affected orders.

Production Scheduling

Finite capacity scheduling across work centers, machines, and labor pools. Visual drag-and-drop tools let planners optimize sequences in real-time.

Stop overbooking your bottleneck work centers and start delivering realistic promise dates.

Inventory & Warehouse Management

Real-time inventory across all locations with lot tracking, bin management, and automatic reorder triggers based on actual consumption.

05

Quality Control & Inspection

Inspection plans tied to operations, with automatic hold-for-inspection triggers and non-conformance tracking built into the production flow.

Catch defects at the operation, not at final inspection—reducing rework and scrap costs.

06

Cost Tracking & Analysis

Track actual material, labor, and overhead costs against standards in real-time. Variance reports by job, product line, or work center.

Know your true cost of production and make pricing decisions with confidence.
