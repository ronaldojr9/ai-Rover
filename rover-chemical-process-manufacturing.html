The Process Manufacturing Challenge

When Formulas Replace Bills of Materials

Process manufacturers don't assemble parts—they blend, mix, react, and transform raw materials into products. Batch sizes flex, yields vary, and co-products and by-products add layers of complexity that discrete manufacturing ERP systems can't comprehend.

⚗️

Formula-Based Production

Recipes with variable yields, unit-of-measure conversions, and potency adjustments. Discrete BOMs can't handle the fluid nature of process manufacturing.

📊

Batch Control & Traceability

Every batch needs a complete record—ingredients, quantities, process parameters, and quality test results. Regulatory agencies demand instant access.

📉

Yield Variability

Actual yields rarely match theoretical. Without real-time tracking, you don't know if a batch is off-spec until it's too late to adjust.

⚠️

Regulatory & Safety Compliance

EPA, OSHA, REACH, SDS management—the regulatory burden for chemical manufacturers is enormous and the penalties for non-compliance are severe.

How Rover Solves It

Precise Formulas. Controlled Batches. Full Compliance.

01

Formula & Recipe Management

Define formulas with variable batch sizes, potency adjustments, and automatic unit-of-measure conversions. Scale production up or down with accurate ingredient calculations.

Produce any batch size with confidence that your formula is correct and costs are calculated.

02

Batch Process Control

Record every step of the production process—ingredient additions, mixing times, temperatures, and operator actions—into a complete electronic batch record.

Release batches faster with complete, accurate records that satisfy regulatory requirements.

03

Yield Tracking & Optimization

Monitor actual vs. theoretical yield in real-time. Track co-products and by-products as valuable outputs, not waste.

Turn yield data into process improvements that increase throughput and reduce material costs.

04

SDS & Regulatory Management

Maintain Safety Data Sheets, manage hazardous material classifications, and track regulatory requirements by product and destination.

Ship with confidence knowing every product has current SDS documentation and proper hazmat classification.

05

Quality & Lab Integration

Define quality test requirements by product and production stage. Record lab results directly into batch records with automatic pass/fail determination.

Catch out-of-spec conditions early and prevent non-conforming product from reaching customers.

06

Inventory with Potency & Lot Control

Track raw materials by lot, potency, and expiration. Automatic potency adjustments ensure formula accuracy regardless of ingredient variability.

Use every lot optimally by adjusting quantities based on actual potency—reducing waste and improving consistency.
