Intelligence Where It Matters

Inventory Optimization

Identify dead stock, predict stockouts, and optimize reorder points based on actual demand patterns—not gut feel.

"Rover identified $180K in slow-moving inventory we'd been sitting on for 2 years. We liquidated 60% of it in the first quarter."

Demand Forecasting

See demand shifts before they hit. Rover analyzes historical patterns, seasonality, and customer behavior to predict what's coming.

"We caught a 40% demand spike two weeks early. First time we weren't scrambling to expedite materials."
