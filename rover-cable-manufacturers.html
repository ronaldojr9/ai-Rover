The Cable Manufacturing Challenge

When Every Foot of Material Counts

Cable manufacturers operate on tight tolerances and tighter margins. Managing continuous-length inventory, tracking yields across production runs, and quoting against complex specifications requires precision that generic ERP systems simply can't deliver.

📏

Continuous-Length Inventory

Cable isn't counted in pieces—it's measured in feet, meters, and reels. Generic inventory systems can't handle cut-to-length operations or partial spool tracking.

⚖️

Material Yield Management

Copper, aluminum, and polymer costs fluctuate daily. Without precise yield tracking, you're losing margin on every production run without knowing it.

📐

Specification Complexity

Customers order by gauge, insulation type, voltage rating, jacket material, and certification requirements. Quoting accurately requires instant access to spec data.

🔍

Quality & Traceability

Every foot of cable needs lot traceability from raw material receipt through finished goods. When a quality issue surfaces, you need to trace back instantly.
