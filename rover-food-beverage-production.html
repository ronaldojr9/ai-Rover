The Food & Beverage Challenge

When Safety and Freshness Can't Be Compromised

Food and beverage manufacturers face unique pressures that most ERP systems aren't built to handle—perishable ingredients, strict regulatory requirements, allergen management, and the constant need for complete traceability from farm to fork.

🔍

Traceability & Recall Readiness

FSMA requires you to trace ingredients forward and backward within hours. Paper-based systems can't keep up, and the cost of a slow recall is catastrophic.

📋

Recipe & Formula Management

Scaling recipes, managing ingredient substitutions, and maintaining consistency across batches while optimizing for yield and cost.

⏰

Shelf Life & Freshness

Managing FEFO (First Expired, First Out) across multiple products and warehouse locations. Expired inventory means waste and lost revenue.

📜

Regulatory Compliance

FDA, HACCP, SQF, organic certifications—each with documentation requirements that consume your quality team's time.

How Rover Solves It

From Recipe to Retail—Safe, Compliant, Profitable

01

Full Lot Traceability

Track every ingredient from receiving through production to finished goods shipment. One-click forward and backward traceability for any lot number.

Respond to recall simulations in minutes instead of hours—and pass every mock recall test.

02

Recipe & Formula Management

Define and scale recipes with automatic yield calculations, allergen tracking, and nutritional rollups. Manage substitutions without compromising quality.

Produce consistently at any batch size while maintaining exact specifications.

03

Shelf Life & FEFO Management

Automatic shelf-life calculation at production. FEFO allocation ensures oldest-dated inventory ships first. Alerts before product approaches expiration.

Minimize waste by keeping your freshest products moving and catching expiring stock early.

04

Quality & HACCP Compliance

Critical control point monitoring built into production workflows. Automatic hold triggers when parameters exceed limits. Complete CCP documentation.

Maintain HACCP and SQF compliance as a natural part of production, not a separate paperwork exercise.

05

Allergen Management

Track allergens at the ingredient level with automatic propagation to finished goods. Production scheduling avoids cross-contamination through line sequencing.

Protect consumers and your brand with systematic allergen control from receiving to labeling.

06

Yield & Cost Optimization

Track actual vs. theoretical yield by recipe, line, and shift. Identify variances in real-time and optimize ingredient costs against market pricing.

Know your true cost per unit and find margin opportunities hidden in your production data.
