The Medical Device Challenge

When Patient Safety Is the Standard

Medical device manufacturers operate under one of the most regulated environments in manufacturing. Every device must be traceable, every process validated, and every record complete—because at the end of the supply chain is a patient.

📋

Device History Records

FDA requires a complete Device History Record for every unit produced. Assembling DHRs manually is time-consuming, error-prone, and audit-risky.

🔐

21 CFR Part 11 Compliance

Electronic records and signatures must meet strict FDA requirements for data integrity, audit trails, and access controls.

⚕️

CAPA & Non-Conformance

Corrective and Preventive Actions must be tracked systematically with root cause analysis, effectiveness verification, and complete documentation.

🏷️

UDI & Traceability

Unique Device Identification requirements demand serialization, labeling accuracy, and GUDID database submissions for every product.

How Rover Solves It

Every Device Traced. Every Record Complete. Every Audit Passed.

01

Automated Device History Records

DHRs compiled automatically from production data—material lots, process parameters, inspection results, and operator records—all linked to the device serial number.

Eliminate manual DHR assembly and produce audit-ready records as a natural output of production.

21 CFR Part 11 Compliance

Electronic signatures with configurable approval workflows. Complete audit trails for every record change. Role-based access controls enforced system-wide.

Meet FDA electronic records requirements without bolting on a separate compliance system.

CAPA & Quality Management

Integrated CAPA system with automated workflows for initiation, investigation, root cause analysis, corrective action, and effectiveness verification.

Close CAPAs faster with structured workflows that ensure nothing falls through the cracks.

UDI & Serialization

Generate and manage UDI labels, track serialized devices through production, and maintain data for GUDID submissions.
